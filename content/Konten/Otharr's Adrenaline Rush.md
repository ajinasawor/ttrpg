**Ring, Wondrous Item, Uncommon, Requires Attunement** 

#### **High Tide**
Once per day, allowed wearer to cast spells faster than normal, allowed spell level 2 or lower with 1 action duration to be casted as bonus action

#### **Low tide**
Once per short REST or long REST, you can delayed cast level 2 spell or lower that casted using spell slot from your prepared spell list, that can be then casted using your reaction. You must declare the condition of which the spell will trigger. The delayed spell gone after 1 hour if unused, and the spell slot still consumed.
